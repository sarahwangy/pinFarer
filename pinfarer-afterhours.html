Pinfarer How It Works
4-Step Flow
Pin. Explore.
Plan. Go.
01
Pin a place
Click the globe, search, or import a KML. Set status: Visited, Watchlist, Dream. Add your source.
02
AI enriches it
Claude Sonnet generates an introduction — population, language, climate, food culture, visa, safety in one click.
03
See your world
Dashboard shows KPI cards, status donut, source breakdown, and country ranking. Your travel life at a glance.
04
Claude plans it
Select saved pins, set length and style. Claude reads your library and returns a day-by-day itinerary with a live map route.
